There are people who still live there, our Indigenous brothers and sisters of the Arawak people — there are few of us left. We do not speak the language now, because there was also a...

EL CONSULADO (00:29)
a rule in the home: the elders were speaking, and you could not sit there the way we are talking now and have someone else come to pry or to listen in. No, no, no — the conversation is not with you, you had to withdraw from there, you do not hear what they say, you do not know what they are saying. Well, that was the law we faced when we were children.

EL CONSULADO (00:58)
But even so, with the work of the home, the planting and all that, we did watch, we had to take part in that work in order to know how you sow, how the moon is, how the rain is, all of that.
